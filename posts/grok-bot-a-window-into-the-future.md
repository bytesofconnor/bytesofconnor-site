Over the past month or so, I've spent a lot of time exploring with 

Feels like a Monopoly

When grok bot went down, or usage ran up out of nowhere, everything came to a total halt. Productivity is so uber high with it, and when power gets cut dry it almost doesn't make sense to try to do just one thing, because the product is so much less than before.

This has helped me better understand the strong proponents of open intellgence. If my business was operating fully on one provider such as grok bot, or muse for that matter, my businesss is completely depedent on that intelligence provider. In a perfect world, intelligence should be available to everyone, and it limitless quantities. But today, this is a significant reason for designing systems that can withhold the stresss of multiple AI providers stopping working at any given time. That is the new kind of "uptime" that is not necesssarily solved. 

I want unlimited intelligence, that has a reasonable degree of ability to get things done and figure out where to find information. It doesn't necessarily need to be the smartest machine out there, I just want it to be able to teach itself anything. I want my machine to do great work reliably, and focus on learning from past mistakes.
